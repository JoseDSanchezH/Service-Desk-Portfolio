## Steps Taken

1. One piece of advice that I was always given was to think the worst of the worst, but work your way outside to go in. For example, the troubleshooting performed by the user, they already checked all the cables that are plugged in; they also swapped in a different cable and tried the other video port on their PC, and there was still no signal. Then they restarted the computer, and they even borrowed A coworker's laptop to test the monitor, and it worked on theirs. Meaning this had to be an issue on their own computer which leads me to believe that it was a graphics card driver problem. I decided to remote desktop my way into Laura Santos computer.

<img width="482" height="486" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0a957ee1-0c66-4eac-a1ba-01d0b4806c93" />



2. I could not take a screenshot of what I did, but I checked for updates on the OS, and there were up to 5 updates that needed to be installed. After updating their computer and rebooting, their integrated graphics driver was updated, which led to their second monitor working.

<img width="986" height="670" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3851990b-8624-4598-87c1-1c4d814bee34" />

## Ticket Notes

- Suspected a graphics driver problem.
- Remoted into Laura Santos computer and checked for OS updates. 5 updates were pending.
- Installed the updates and rebooted. The integrated graphics driver was updated.
- Second monitor now working.
