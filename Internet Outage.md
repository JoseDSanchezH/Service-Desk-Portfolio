## Internet Outage

# Ticket Information
INC0012858 (Critical): Matt Reeves, facility, floor three:  no internet on floor 3. 

# Steps Taken

1. Open the ticket and read the details. Only floor 3 is affected, so the problem is specific to floor 3.

<img width="558" height="462" alt="Nobody has internet on the 3rd floor  2026-10-20 112208" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2033c38e-b654-44d8-9d7f-89a5248d8066" />

2. Open the server room and check for devices that control floor 3.

3. I was able to find that floor three had a switch, and it was offline with an error. The other devices were online. The screenshot below is what I took after I solved it. This is just for reference; I forgot to take a screenshot of what I saw, so that is on me. 


<img width="438" height="490" alt="Before fixing it it did say offline and error 2026-10-20 112243" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/27d53e99-7e7d-4b2c-a34b-bab6e9e81c40" />

4. I was able to reboot the Floor 3 switch from the actions menu.

<img width="932" height="746" alt="clicked reboot on the floor 3 switch and it went back online  2026-10-20 112354" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b52b85db-f725-42ea-8cac-1cf443c82f42" />

5. I then checked the device status, and the switch was online. All eight out of eight devices were up and running, and the switch time was set to 0 days.

6. I closed the ticket with the resolution notes.
