# Goals


My goal is to gain more hands-on experience as a help desk troubleshooter. 
Learning on the job does help, but with more research, I found different simulations that will help me improve my troubleshooting skills.
The more I practice different troubleshooting problems, the more I feel comfortable with being a help desk agent and answering interview questions. 

I have to give credit to: https://servicedesk-simulator.com/

I've been learning how to troubleshoot certain problems that I might see in the real world. 
Thankfully, I can work on the simulation and become better at troubleshooting on my own. 

All of the tickets, names, IP Addresses, and emails here are simulated. No real company or customer data is included. 

For each ticket, I include: 
- the original ticket
- my steps taken with screenshots
- short ticket notes, the way I would write them on the job.
