# Steps Taken


1. She told me she tried emptying the trash and deleted some old files, but she's still getting notified "Low Disk Space". I decided to Remote into her laptop and check her local disk.

2. I then went to Properties and selected "Temporary files, " " System Update Cleanup, " " Trash, " and Temporary Internet Files and used Disk Cleanup to free up 29 GB.
